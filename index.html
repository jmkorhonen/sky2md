Everything happens in your browser: the only network requests go to Bluesky's public API, and images in the preview load from Bluesky's CDN. Posts whose authors ask to be shown only to logged-in users are not converted. This page is a single self-contained file — download it and it works offline from your disk (internet is still needed to fetch threads). Tip: drag → sky2md to your bookmarks bar, then click it while viewing a Bluesky post to convert its thread. · Source
