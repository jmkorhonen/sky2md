Everything happens in your browser: the only network requests go to Bluesky's public API, and images in the preview load from Bluesky's CDN. Your option choices are saved in this browser only, and stay there until you clear its site data. Posts whose authors ask to be shown only to logged-in users are not converted. This page is a single self-contained file — download it and it works offline from your disk (internet is still needed to fetch threads). Tip: drag → sky2md to your bookmarks bar, then click it while viewing a Bluesky post to convert its thread. · Source
